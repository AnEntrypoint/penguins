# African Penguin

## Key Facts

- **Medium-sized penguin** - adults reach 2.0-2.3 feet tall and weigh 5-8 pounds
- **Habitat**: Coastal South Africa and Namibia; the only penguin species breeding on the African continent
- **Also known as**: Jackass penguin or Black-footed penguin, for its loud donkey-like braying call
- **Scientific name**: *Spheniscus demersus*
- **Distinctive markings**: Single black band across the chest, pink glands above the eyes used for thermoregulation
- **Diet**: Pelagic schooling fish, especially sardines and anchovies; squid taken opportunistically
- **Breeding**: Nests in burrows dug into guano, under boulders, or in artificial nest boxes deployed by conservation programs
- **Population collapse**: Numbers have fallen from roughly 1 million pairs in 1900 to under 10,000 pairs today — a decline of over 99%
- **Primary threats**: Industrial fishery competition for forage fish, oil spills, climate-driven prey shifts, historical guano harvesting
- **Lifespan**: 10-15 years in the wild, up to 30 in managed care
- **Conservation status**: Critically Endangered (uplisted by IUCN in 2024); SANCCOB rescue and the boulders colony at Boulders Beach are flagship programs
