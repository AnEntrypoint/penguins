# Chinstrap Penguin

## Key Facts

- **Medium-sized penguin** - adults reach 2.3-2.5 feet tall and weigh 7-11 pounds
- **Habitat**: Antarctic Peninsula, South Sandwich Islands, South Orkney, South Shetland, and South Georgia
- **Named after**: The thin black band of feathers under the chin that gives the appearance of a helmet strap
- **Scientific name**: *Pygoscelis antarcticus*
- **Distinctive markings**: White face with the diagnostic chin band; black back, white belly
- **Diet**: Almost exclusively Antarctic krill, with small amounts of fish and squid
- **Population**: One of the most numerous penguin species at roughly 8 million individuals
- **Diving ability**: Typically dives 20-40 m for under a minute; capable of 70 m dives
- **Breeding**: Lays two eggs in November on ice-free rocky slopes; both parents incubate for ~37 days
- **Lifespan**: 15-20 years in the wild
- **Conservation status**: Least Concern overall, but Antarctic Peninsula colonies are declining sharply with krill loss
