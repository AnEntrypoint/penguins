# King Penguin

## Key Facts

- **Second-largest penguin species** - adults reach 2.8-3.1 feet tall and weigh 20-40 pounds
- **Habitat**: Sub-Antarctic islands including South Georgia, Crozet, Kerguelen, Prince Edward, Heard, Macquarie
- **Scientific name**: *Aptenodytes patagonicus*
- **Distinctive markings**: Black head with vivid orange ear-patches and a golden-orange wash across the upper chest
- **Diet**: Primarily lanternfish (myctophids); also squid and small crustaceans
- **Diving ability**: Routinely dives 100-300 m; recorded to 343 m, with dives lasting up to 9 minutes
- **Breeding**: Unique 14-16 month breeding cycle; successful pairs raise only two chicks every three years
- **Incubation**: Single egg balanced on the feet under a brood pouch; both parents share 18-day shifts for ~55 days
- **Chick rearing**: Brown woolly chicks famously fast through winter, sometimes for 5 months between parental feeds
- **Lifespan**: Up to 26 years in the wild
- **Conservation status**: Least Concern globally, but the world's largest colony on Ile aux Cochons has crashed since the 1980s
