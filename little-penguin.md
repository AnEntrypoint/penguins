# Little Penguin

## Key Facts

- **Smallest penguin species** - adults stand 12-16 inches tall and weigh 2-3 pounds
- **Also known as**: Fairy penguin, Little Blue penguin, korora (Maori)
- **Habitat**: Coastal southern Australia, Tasmania, and New Zealand
- **Scientific name**: *Eudyptula minor*
- **Distinctive markings**: Slate-blue back and flippers with a clean white belly — the only blue penguin
- **Diet**: Small schooling fish (anchovies, pilchards), squid, and krill taken in shallow coastal waters
- **Diving ability**: Mostly shallow dives under 20 m; foraging trips usually return the same day
- **Breeding**: Nests in burrows, rock crevices, and under coastal vegetation; lays two eggs, often raises two clutches per season
- **Social behavior**: Comes ashore after dusk in groups called "rafts"; the Phillip Island Penguin Parade is a famous tourism site
- **Lifespan**: 6-7 years typical, up to 20 in protected populations
- **Conservation status**: Least Concern overall; local declines from dogs, foxes, vehicle strikes, and oil pollution
