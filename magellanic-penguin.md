# Magellanic Penguin

## Key Facts

- **Medium-sized penguin** - adults reach 2.0-2.5 feet tall and weigh 6-14 pounds
- **Habitat**: Coastal Argentina, Chile, and the Falkland Islands; winters as far north as southern Brazil and Peru
- **Named after**: Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan, who first recorded the species in 1520
- **Scientific name**: *Spheniscus magellanicus*
- **Distinctive markings**: Two black bands across the chest (the outer one horseshoe-shaped) and a white facial stripe
- **Diet**: Schooling fish including anchovies, sardines, and hake; also squid
- **Migration**: Strongly migratory; tracked individuals travel thousands of kilometers north for the austral winter
- **Breeding**: Excavates burrows or nests under shrubs at large mainland colonies such as Punta Tombo (Argentina), where over 200,000 pairs gather
- **Threats**: Chronic oil pollution from tanker traffic along the South American coast; shifting prey distribution from climate change
- **Lifespan**: 25-30 years in the wild
- **Conservation status**: Least Concern, but classified as a species of concern locally where colonies are declining
